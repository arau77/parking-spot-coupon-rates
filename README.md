**Why didn't my cash back track?**
The usual suspects: you weren't on a portal's link with an empty cart, you used Safari, or you applied a coupon code the portal doesn't list. **What's the Reservation Arrival Window?**
It's the span when your reservation is honored: from 9 PM the day before your check-in date until your checkout time on Pay Now bookings. A July 3 reservation can be entered starting July 2 at 9 PM. Pay Later runs slightly longer, until 3 AM the day after your reservation date.

**Is The Parking Spot cheaper than airport parking?**
Usually, and sometimes by a wide margin. On-airport economy lots at major airports commonly run well above the $6.50–$21 range The Parking Spot publishes, and the trade-off is a shuttle ride instead of a walk to the terminal.
